The Neon Larks

The Neon Larks are a fictional indie synth-rock four-piece from Portland, Oregon, invented for this demo site. The lineup pairs shimmering keyboard lines with a rhythm section that grew up on garage rock, and the contrast became their signature: songs that feel like a late-night drive with the windows down. They spent their first two years playing basement venues and open-mic nights before a homemade EP started circulating farther than they ever expected.

Their debut full-length, Glass Horizon, leans into that momentum — bigger choruses, brighter synths, and lyrics about leaving small towns without leaving them behind. Fans call themselves “the Flock,” and the band closes every show the same way they opened their very first one: unplugged, lights up, singing with the room instead of at it. A second record is already taking shape between tour stops.
